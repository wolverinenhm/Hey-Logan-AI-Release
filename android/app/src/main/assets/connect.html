Hey Logan AI

Connect this phone to the bridge on your PC.

Tip: paste the whole pairing link from the Pairing window into the first field. The app fills in the token and connects.

Where do I find these?

  1. On your PC, start Hey Logan AI from the Start Menu and keep it running.
  2. Open Pairing (Pairing.cmd in the Start Menu): it shows the phone address (for example https://192.168.1.20:8443), the access token and a QR code.
  3. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the PC.
  4. Your PC uses a private, self-signed certificate. The app asks once and then trusts it for that PC only.
